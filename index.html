Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, authoritative answers to Indian taxation, corporate registration, and statutory notice questions.

For businesses engaged solely in the supply of Goods, GST registration is mandatory when aggregate turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs (₹20 Lakhs for Special Category States). For Service Providers, the turnover limit is ₹20 Lakhs (₹10 Lakhs in Special Category States). Regardless of turnover, GST is compulsory for inter-state suppliers and e-commerce sellers (Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify).
A Private Limited Company is recommended if you plan to raise venture capital, pitch to angel investors, or issue employee stock options (ESOPs). A Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) is ideal for consulting firms, agencies, and bootstrap ventures because it combines limited liability with lower annual statutory maintenance costs.
Do not panic, but do not ignore it. Notice ASMT-10 typically highlights differences between GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B or turnover discrepancies. You typically have 15 to 30 days to submit a factual explanation. Contact Maraqs Commerce immediately—we audit the discrepancies, collect vendor proof, and upload a legally drafted reply.
Section 44ADA allows individual professionals (freelancers, developers, lawyers, doctors) with gross receipts up to ₹75 Lakhs to declare 50% of receipts as profit and pay tax without maintaining complex ledgers or undergoing tax audit. Section 44AD offers 6% or 8% presumptive rates for small trading businesses with turnover up to ₹3 Crore.
