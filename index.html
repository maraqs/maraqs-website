Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to basic questions regarding GST, ITR, and Business Registrations.

When is GST registration mandatory in India? ▼

GST registration is compulsory for businesses selling goods whose turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs (₹20 Lakhs for Special Category States). For service providers, the threshold is ₹20 Lakhs (₹10 Lakhs in Special Category States). E-commerce sellers and businesses engaged in inter-state sales require mandatory GST registration regardless of turnover.

What documents are required for Private Limited Company registration? ▼

For all proposed directors: PAN card, Aadhaar card/Passport/Voter ID, and updated bank statement/electricity bill (under 2 months old). For the registered office: Electricity bill or property tax receipt along with a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner.

What is the difference between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B? ▼

GSTR-1 is a monthly or quarterly return where you declare details of all outward supplies (sales). GSTR-3B is a monthly self-declaration summary return used to report summary values of sales, claim eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC), and remit the net tax payable to the government.

What should I do if I receive a GST notice? ▼

Do not ignore the notice. Check the DIN (Document Identification Number) to verify its authenticity, evaluate the exact reason (such as GSTR-3B vs GSTR-2B mismatch, ASMT-10, or late filing), and draft a factual, documented response within the stipulated 15 to 30 day window. Maraqs Commerce handles notice analysis and legal drafted replies.

Can I file my Income Tax Return without Form 16? ▼

Yes. You can file using your monthly salary slips, your bank account statements, your AIS (Annual Information Statement), and Form 26AS to cross-verify all TDS deducted by your employer.
