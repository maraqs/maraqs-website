About Maraqs Commerce

8+ Years of Trusted Corporate Governance & Tax Advisory

Established with a vision to simplify corporate compliance, Maraqs Commerce Pvt Ltd delivers reliable accounting, tax defense, and business licensing across India.

Professional Heritage & Values

Founded over 8 years ago, Maraqs Commerce Pvt Ltd has grown from a specialized tax boutique in Ghaziabad into a comprehensive, multi-disciplinary corporate consulting firm. We serve emerging startups, proprietorships, MSMEs, and established corporate companies across Delhi-NCR and PAN India.

Our approach blends deep domain knowledge of Indian taxation (Income Tax, GST, TDS) and company laws (Companies Act 2013, MCA V3) with cutting-edge digital document workflows, ensuring complete transparency, zero billing surprises, and guaranteed statutory timeliness.
